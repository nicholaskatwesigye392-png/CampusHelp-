👋 Welcome to CampusHelp Help Center

Need help using CampusHelp? This page explains how to report problems, track reports and understand notifications.

📝 How to Report a Problem

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Open the Report a Problem page.
  2. Select the type of problem.
  3. Enter the location of the problem.
  4. Describe the problem clearly.
  5. Submit your report.
  6. Keep the Report ID provided by CampusHelp.
💡 Tip: Always provide accurate information so that the responsible campus department can understand and respond to the problem.

🔎 How to Track a Report

After submitting a report, you can check its progress using your Report ID.

  1. Open Track My Report.
  2. Enter your Report ID.
  3. Press the Track Report button.
  4. View the current status of your report.
📌 Remember: Keep your Report ID safe because it helps you identify your report.

🔔 Understanding Notifications

CampusHelp can notify you when the status of your report changes.

  • New: Your report has been received.
  • In Progress: The report is being worked on.
  • Resolved: The reported problem has been addressed.
🔔 Important: Check your CampusHelp home page regularly to see new notifications about your report.

🔐 Login & Account Help

If you are having problems logging into CampusHelp:

  • Check that your username is correct.
  • Check that your password is correct.
  • Make sure you select the correct user role.
  • If access is denied, return to the Login page and try again.

🛡️ Campus Safety

CampusHelp is designed to help students report campus problems. Always provide truthful and accurate information when submitting a report.

📞 Need More Help?

If you cannot solve your problem using this Help Center, contact the appropriate campus office or staff member for assistance.
