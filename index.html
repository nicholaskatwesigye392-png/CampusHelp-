About CampusHelp

CampusHelp is a student-focused campus problem reporting and support platform. It provides a simple way for students to report campus issues, monitor the progress of submitted reports and access important campus updates.


Students can report issues related to campus safety, electricity, water, internet, infrastructure and other campus services. The platform helps connect students with the appropriate campus staff for follow-up and action.


CampusHelp is designed to make campus communication easier, faster and more organized.
